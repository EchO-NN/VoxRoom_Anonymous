Method

From geometric evidence
to room boundaries.

VoxRoom aggregates free-space evidence across voxel heights to reduce furniture-induced fragmentation. Complementary 3D and 2D cues propose entrances, which are verified before becoming room separators.

01

Accumulate geometry

Fuse depth or LiDAR observations and poses into a voxel map with free, occupied, and unknown states.

0.05 m voxels
02

Extract structure

Build the Structural Free Map. Take the union of candidates from 3D column screening and 2D ray casting.

3D candidates ∪ 2D candidates
03

Verify entry seeds

A dual-branch network combines a local voxel patch with wider planar context to score each candidate.

19 × 19 × Z + 41 × 41
04

Separate rooms

Cluster verified seeds, fit and extend separator segments, then extract connected room regions.

Entry score ≥ 0.5

The Structural Free Map represents structural connectivity. Navigation uses a separate Nav-Free Map. Unobserved regions remain unassigned.
