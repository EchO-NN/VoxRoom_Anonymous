VoxRoom: Room Segmentation from Partial Observations during Robot Exploration

Anonymous Authors

▶ Watch overviewSource code ↓

VoxRoom is an online room-segmentation method for robots exploring partially observed, cluttered environments. Its Structural Free Map aggregates free-space evidence across voxel heights, while complementary 3D and 2D entrance candidates are verified before rooms are separated. Trained only in simulation, the verifier transfers directly to real robots without fine-tuning.

Method

From geometric evidence
to room boundaries.

VoxRoom aggregates free-space evidence across voxel heights to reduce furniture-induced fragmentation. Complementary 3D and 2D cues propose entrances, which are verified before becoming room separators.

The Structural Free Map represents structural connectivity. Navigation uses a separate Nav-Free Map. Unobserved regions remain unassigned.
